# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Design hiring managers, recruiters, and product or engineering leads evaluating Naveen Kumar for senior product design work. They scan first (who, what kind of work, which decisions) and read in depth only when a case study earns it.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Naveen Kumar, Product Designer. Success: within the first viewport a reader knows who Naveen is, what kind of products the work covers, what makes the approach different, and where to see the work. Each case study proves product judgment (constraints, decisions, trade-offs, systems thinking), not familiarity with tools.

## Positioning

Product Designer building AI-powered SaaS products, agentic experiences, and scalable enterprise workflows: blending engineering thinking, UX, and AI to simplify complex workflows into products people can actually understand.

## Operating Context

- Static Next.js export served from Firebase Hosting (`naveen2026-portfolio`).
- Case studies are local structured content in `src/content/case-studies/`, rendered by one shared narrative format (`StoryCaseStudy`).
- Readers arrive on desktop and mobile, in light or dark system themes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4 over CSS-variable tokens, Motion, Lucide. Server components by default.
- `Md Files/PORTFOLIO_FRONTEND_RULES.md` is the implementation source of truth.
- The résumé is an external Google Doc linked from the navigation. An About page does not exist yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Naveen Kumar.
- Direction "Warm Tech Futurism": light theme "Optimistic Daylight", dark theme "Quiet Future" (rules file §9, §29). Never cyberpunk, neon, crypto-dashboard, or purple SaaS gradients.
- Voice: first person, plain, specific about decisions and trade-offs.

## Product Principles

1. Prove judgment, not tooling: show the decision, its reason, and its trade-off.
2. Truth over impressiveness: unverified claims are left out, not softened into implication.
3. Scan path first, depth on demand: the opening answers the reader's first question; detail follows.
4. One system: every case study uses the same narrative format and design tokens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Rules file §19: semantic HTML, correct heading order, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, useful alt text, reduced-motion support, and nothing critical behind hover. Meaning is never carried by colour alone.
